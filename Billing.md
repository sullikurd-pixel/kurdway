# Free and Premium subscriptions
Free: basic maps, trail discovery, community participation and essential safety information. Premium: proposed advanced offline packs, planning and statistics. Monthly and annual recurring plans; prices undecided. Stripe Checkout and Customer Portal are candidates for web, subject to country/provider eligibility, sanctions compliance and merchant approval. Native app purchases must follow applicable store billing policies. Use test mode first. Server selects allowlisted price IDs; signed webhook validation and idempotent processing; reconcile subscription state and grant entitlements server-side, never from redirect or client input. Support cancel, renew, failed payment, refund and expiry. Do not store card details. Keep admin role independent of subscription. Free access must remain available where lawful and practical; never bypass geographic restrictions.
