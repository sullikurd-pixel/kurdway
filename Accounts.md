# Account architecture
Proposed: Supabase Auth email OTP with custom SMTP, PostgreSQL RLS and server-side authorization; validate against existing local app before adoption. Email is mandatory and verified. Six-digit single-use code, short expiry (target 5 minutes), resend cooldown, per-account/IP rate limits and generic responses; never log codes. First verified login creates a free user. Admin role is separate from plan, assigned only by trusted server-side operator, with mandatory MFA and audit logging. WhatsApp via approved Meta authentication templates later, opt-in and verified linking to existing email account. No real credentials in repository. Local development uses synthetic users and local mail capture, production uses verified sender domain. Tests: expired/replayed codes, enumeration, rate limits, cross-account access and role escalation. References: https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/auth-email-passwordless and https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/auth-smtp
